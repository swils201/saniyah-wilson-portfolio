# About Me   
<img src="PFP" alt="Headshot" width="300"> 

**Hi,** my name is Saniyah Wilson and I am a third-year Mechanical Engineering Major at UNC Charlotte.   
  
Growing up, I moved up and down the East coast, developing a deep appreciation for the systems that allowed the environments around me to hold beauty and efficiency at once. With large interest in art, personal research led me to discover that in almost every step parts are catered to heighten the users experience, a system I connected to the art process. I have since concentrated this interest in product and entertainment product design which has motivated my choice to major in Mechanical Engineering.
   
Having earned my Associates in both Engineering and Science in 2026, I am competent in collaborative teamwork, efficient communication, and key conceptual proficiency in the academic theory product designs are built upon. Highlighting my experience are two courses, Geology 1 and Intro to Ethics, which provided unique insight to the worldly consideration and the critical thinking necessary in Engineering. Over the course of my education, I have demonstrated efficiency in receiving new data, identifying patterns and implementing the necessary problem solving strategies, and crucially, in recognizing the reasoning behind error. These skills have allowed me to earn the Certified SolidWorks Associate certificate. I look forward to growing in both academia and my career going forward.
